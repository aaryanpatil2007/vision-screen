Comprehensive vision screening

A guided ten-part battery covering visual acuity (both eyes and each eye alone), contrast sensitivity, astigmatism, color vision, the central visual field, eye alignment and tracking, pupil light response, and a photorefraction estimate of refractive error. It takes about 10 minutes and runs entirely on this machine — your video is analyzed locally and never leaves it.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and it does not replace an eye exam. It cannot check eye pressure, examine the retina, or detect many diseases. Anything it flags — and any symptom it misses — should be taken to an optometrist.

2 · Calibrate your screen

Hold a credit card (or any ID card) against the screen and drag until the outline matches its width exactly. This converts letter sizes to real angles — without it, acuity numbers are meaningless.

Scale: 37.8 px/cm (card is 8.56 cm wide)

3 · Viewing distance

Measure roughly how far your eyes are from the screen and set it here. Keep this distance for the whole test.

50 cm

4 · Before you start

• Wear your usual glasses or contacts only if you want your corrected vision tested — otherwise remove them.

• Set screen brightness to maximum for the contrast and color tests.

• You'll be asked to cover one eye at a time — use your palm, not your fingers.

• The last two tests need a dark room. You can dim the lights when prompted.
